Part two

On merchandise

Blank products were photographed by an image model (NanoGPT, Qwen Image). The logo was then placed by code from the real SVG, warped to each surface and shaded by the photo, so every mark and letter is exact. Rule of thumb: the lockup on anything with a flat front a hand-width or wider, the mark alone on curved or small items.

Why not let the AI place the logo?

For comparison, the same request given straight to two image models with the logo as a reference. Both get close, and both redraw the letters, which is fine for a mood board but not for a print proof. Canva’s connector returns only a small preview; the full image is in your Canva uploads.
