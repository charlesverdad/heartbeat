Part two

Using the brand in media

These are suggestions to start from, built only from what the logo already gives us: black, white, a tight grotesk, and the three blocky letters. Sample copy is placeholder text; the Bible text is the World English Bible, which is public domain.

Logo

  • Keep clear space on every side equal to the height of the C, which is half the mark’s height.
  • Smallest sizes: mark 24 px tall on screen, lockup 44 px tall. Below that the wordmark stops being readable.
  • Logo is only ever pure black or pure white. Over photos, sit it on a dark scrim or a solid block.

Colour

Ink#000000Logo, headlines, backgrounds
Paper#FFFFFFLogo, text on ink
Bone#F2EFEALarge light surfaces
Graphite#1C1C1CPanels on ink
Stone#8A8782Labels, secondary text

The website already uses black as its only accent. If a series needs its own colour, give that colour to the series artwork and keep the logo black or white.

Type

Inter Tight Bold
Inter Tight Medium for body copy, verses and lyrics.
Labels · uppercase · +14% tracking
  • Inter Tight is free on Google Fonts and is the closest open match to the wordmark. Install it on every machine that runs PowerPoint, Keynote or ProPresenter, or slides will fall back to Arial.
  • Headlines: Bold, tracking −3%, line spacing about 0.95. Body: Medium, normal tracking.
  • Two weights only. Don’t use Light or italics on screens people read from a distance.

Graphic devices

  • The echo. Outlined copies of the mark radiating out, the still frame of the Pulse animation. Use it large and cropped off an edge on title slides, thumbnails and series art.
  • The trace. The heart-monitor line from the ECG animation. Use it as a divider on event graphics. One per layout.
  • The step. H, B and C stack down a diagonal. Let layouts follow it: headline top-left, detail lower and to the right.

Avoid

  • Recolouring, outlining, stretching, rotating or adding shadows to the logo.
  • Re-setting “Heartbeat Church” in a font next to the mark. Use the lockup file.
  • Gradients, glows or stock textures behind text.

Channel

Sermon slides

16:9 at 1920 × 1080. Black for the title and scripture, so the room stays dark during the message; bone for points and announcements. Left-align everything, keep 140 px margins, and put the mark bottom-right on every slide after the first.

  • One idea per slide. A point heading is two lines at most.
  • Scripture: reference and translation as a label above, the verse in Medium at 70–80 px. Split long passages across slides.
  • Number points only when the sermon actually counts them.
  • Open the deck with the Slide reveal or End card animation exported at 16:9.
Channel

ProPresenter & livestream

Overlays are transparent PNGs at 1920 × 1080, built to sit on the camera feed. Keep everything inside a 96 px title-safe margin and in the bottom third, so faces stay clear. The previews below use a stand-in stage; the PNGs in the repo have no background.

  • Lyrics: two lines, Bold, at least 60 px, on a black fade rising from the bottom edge.
  • Name tag: white tile with the black mark, then name and role. Show it for 6–8 seconds at the start of a talk.
  • Bug: the mark top-right at 70% opacity for the whole stream.
  • Pre-service: the holding screen with the Heartbeat loop animation behind or beside the countdown.
